AgentVault — Live Demo

Registry, secret management and bounded execution for autonomous AI agents. Apache-2.0.

What it does

  • Registry — catalogue of agent tools with policies and capabilities.
  • Secret management — scoped secret store with Argon2 hashing.
  • Bounded execution — policy engine, rate limiting, RBAC, capability restriction.
  • Audit trail — append-only evidence of every tool call and approval.
